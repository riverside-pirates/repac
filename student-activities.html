Student Activities

Riverside engineering students have many opportunities to grow beyond the classroom through competitions, community service, and social events.

Technology Student Association (TSA)

TSA is a national student organization for students enrolled in STEM courses. Riverside's TSA chapter is one of the most active in North Carolina, consistently sending students to regional, state, and national competitions.

TSA competitions cover a wide range of events including:

  • Engineering design challenges
  • Architectural design
  • Coding and software development
  • Video production and digital media
  • Structural engineering (bridge and tower building)
  • Transportation modeling
  • Debating technological issues
All engineering students are encouraged to join TSA! It's a great way to apply classroom skills, build a portfolio, and have fun competing with students from across the state and nation.

Community Service

Engineering students participate in a variety of community service projects throughout the year. These projects give students the chance to apply their skills to help the community while fulfilling service hour requirements.

Specific community service projects and volunteer hour requirements to be updated by REPAC leadership and engineering faculty.

Social Events

REPAC organizes social events throughout the year to build community among engineering students and families. Past events have included:

  • Welcome picnics for incoming freshmen
  • End-of-year celebrations
  • Senior recognition events
  • Family gatherings and cookouts

Check the Events page for upcoming social events.
