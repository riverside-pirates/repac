Meeting Schedule

Regular Meetings: 2nd Thursday of each month
Time: 6:00 PM
Location: Riverside High School Media Center

All parents and guardians of Riverside engineering students are encouraged to attend. Meetings typically include updates from the engineering faculty, discussion of upcoming events and fundraising, volunteer coordination, and open Q&A.
