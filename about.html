About REPAC

Our History

REPAC was founded in February 2008 by a group of dedicated parents who saw the need to support Riverside High School's growing pre-engineering program. What started as informal parent gatherings grew into a structured organization committed to ensuring every engineering student has access to the tools, experiences, and mentorship they need.

In 2016, REPAC was incorporated as a North Carolina nonprofit corporation and received 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status from the IRS, allowing donations to be tax-deductible.

Our Mission

REPAC's mission is to support the Project Lead The Way (PLTW) pre-engineering faculty and students at Riverside High School. We achieve this by:

  • Fundraising for equipment, tools, and classroom supplies
  • Coordinating volunteer mentors from the engineering community
  • Facilitating student participation in competitions (TSA, etc.)
  • Building community among engineering families
  • Supporting student activities and social events

Get Involved

Every parent of a Riverside engineering student is automatically a member of REPAC. There are no dues to join. We encourage all families to attend monthly meetings, volunteer for events, and help support our students. Whether you can give time, talent, or treasure, there's a place for you.
