PLTW Course Descriptions

Riverside's PLTW engineering pathway includes the following courses. Students typically progress through foundation courses before advancing to the capstone.

Introduction to Engineering Design (IED)

Typically taken: 9th grade (Freshman year)

IED is the introductory course in the PLTW engineering pathway. Students are introduced to the engineering design process, applying math, science, and engineering standards to identify and design solutions to a variety of real problems. They work both individually and in teams to develop and document design solutions using engineering notebooks and 3D modeling software such as Autodesk Inventor.

Key topics: Design process, technical sketching, 3D solid modeling, reverse engineering, statistics, geometric dimensionality and tolerancing.

Principles of Engineering (POE)

Typically taken: 10th grade (Sophomore year)

POE is a survey course that exposes students to some of the major concepts they'll encounter in a post-secondary engineering course of study. Students explore a broad range of engineering topics including mechanisms, the strength of materials and structures, automation, and kinematics. They develop skills in problem solving, research, and design while learning strategies for design process documentation, collaboration, and presentation.

Key topics: Mechanisms, energy sources, statics, material properties, control systems, kinematics, fluid power, statistics, and thermodynamics.

Civil Engineering & Architecture (CEA)

Typically taken: 11th grade (Junior year)

CEA is a specialization course that introduces students to the fundamental design and development aspects of civil engineering and architecture. Students use professional-grade software (such as Autodesk Revit) to address real-world problems and explore building and site design, including residential and commercial projects.

Key topics: Project planning, site planning, building design, structural analysis, utilities and services, environmental design, and professional practice.

Engineering Design & Development (EDD)

Typically taken: 12th grade (Senior year) — Capstone course

EDD is the capstone course of the PLTW engineering pathway. Students work in teams to design and develop an original solution to a well-defined, open-ended technical problem. They apply the knowledge and skills acquired in previous PLTW courses, research a problem, design a solution, test and refine their prototype, and present their finished product to a panel of outside professionals.

Key topics: Problem identification, research, solution design, prototyping, testing, iteration, technical documentation, professional presentation.

Additional Courses

Additional elective or supplementary engineering courses (e.g., Computer Science, AP Computer Science Principles, Digital Electronics) may be available. Check with RHS guidance counselors for the most current course offerings.
